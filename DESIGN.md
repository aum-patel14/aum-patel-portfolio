# Zen Minimalist Design System

A serene, natural, and calming theme for high-end digital storytelling. Designed to evoke a state of calm focus and clean modern minimalism, avoiding the harsh high-contrast saturated colors of classic tech.

## Colors & Theme
- **Nocturnal Forest Background:** Deepest sage charcoal `#0C0E0C` to ground the canvas.
- **Sage Glass Surfaces:** Translucent modules (`#121613` at 45% opacity) with thin organic borders.
- **Natural Accents:** Organic Tea Green / Sage `#8FBC8F` for core details, graphs, and active states.
- **Calm Typography Tones:** Soft gray-greens for body text and headers to reduce visual stress.
